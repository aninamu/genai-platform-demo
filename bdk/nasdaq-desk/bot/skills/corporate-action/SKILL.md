# Corporate-action break triage

Governed Data Ops workflow. Read the CA bulletin, classify breaks against the
reconciliation SOP, and draft a client notice. Do not mark the notice sent
until a human approves.

## Flow

1. Read the bulletin and the reference-data SOP.
2. Flag Type-A / Type-B / Type-C breaks with proposed fixes.
3. Draft a client notice.
4. Wait for approval; only then mark “sent (demo)”.
