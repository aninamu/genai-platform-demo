# Desktop research → phone result

Matches the mobile idea from the Nasdaq demo prep: launch agentic research in
the platform tab, then check the finished brief on a phone while walking into
a meeting. Voice is shown as pending — it waits on Nasdaq’s mobile app.

## Flow

1. Run a longer research brief on the desktop Agents tab.
2. Persist the session in the browser demo store.
3. Open `/agents/mobile` to read the same result in a phone frame.
4. Keep the Voice control visible but disabled.

## Demo caller

`bdk:mobile-research`
