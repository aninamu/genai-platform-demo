## Interview: Maya Chen, Capital Access desk lead (synthetic)

**Topic:** How we prep a first-time issuer for a non-deal roadshow.

### What is not in any system of record

- Prefer two buy-side accounts that already cover the peer set before pitching
  the issuer’s IR calendar.
- Soft block: if the CFO has never done a group lunch, schedule a 1:1 with a
  friendly long-only first — never open with a hedge-fund panel.
- Pricing conversation: keep “indicative” language until Legal clears the
  deck; desk slang “whisper range” must not appear in email.

### Decision gates

1. Coverage map signed by sector banker (or delegate).
2. Compliance review of any forward-looking slide.
3. IR confirms travel window ≥ 10 business days out.

### Owners

- Desk lead: owns account mix
- Banker: owns issuer relationship
- Compliance: owns language freeze
